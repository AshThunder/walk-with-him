## Notes
- The voices are placeholders. To replace one with a recording, overwrite `audio/voice/<line id>.ogg`; `data/lines.json` maps every line id.
- Not used, on purpose: Synty packs (licence limits redistribution and modification) and the Fab Jerusalem listing.
- Jesus's few invented spoken lines are short narrative bridges and add no doctrine. Scripture is quoted verbatim from the KJV.
